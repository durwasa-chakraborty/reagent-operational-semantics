** Proof Statement
***

$$\forall C'\, \forall \tau\, \forall H.\ \mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C' \wedge \mathit{HistoryOf}\ \tau\ H \rightarrow H \models \mathit{Int} \wedge H \models \mathit{Ext}$$
We proved this for the pessimistic track, but we could change the $\forall$ to
an $\exists$ and hope all our problems disappear.
With $\forall$ we go through every $H$: one of them is the true history and
the rest hold vacuously, which does not matter because $H$ is universally
quantified.
With $\exists$ we can choose an $H$ that makes the antecedent $\bot$ and then
prove wrong things about it.
Certain things will hold vacuously, so we move from $\forall \tau\, \exists H$
to

$$\forall C'\, \forall \tau.\ \mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C' \rightarrow \exists H.\ \mathit{HistoryOf}\ \tau\ H \wedge H \models \mathit{Int} \wedge H \models \mathit{Ext}$$

** Proving HistoryOf

$$\forall C'\, \forall \tau.\ \mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C' \rightarrow \exists H.\ \mathit{HistoryOf}\ \tau\ H$$

*** Witness

We are handed $C'$ and $\tau$ with $\mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C'$, and we have
to come up with a history. H1 already fixes the transactions to those of
$\tau$, so the only thing we actually get to choose is $\mathit{AR}$. We order
transactions by when they finish:
$$H := (\mathit{txs}(\tau),\ \mathit{AR})$$
\begin{align*}
\mathit{AR}(S,T) :=\ & (\mathit{fin}\,S \wedge \mathit{fin}\,T \wedge \mathit{term}_S \prec \mathit{term}_T) \\
\vee\ & (\mathit{fin}\,S \wedge \mathit{pend}\,T) \\
\vee\ & (\mathit{pend}\,S \wedge \mathit{pend}\,T \wedge \mathit{begin}_S \prec \mathit{begin}_T)
\end{align*}


2/3 seems to be obvious except that the last one.
The order among pending transactions is arbitrary. H2 wants a total order, so
they have to go in some order, and $\mathsf{begin}$ order is simply an easy
one. It says nothing about how they will end: if the run goes on and $T$
commits before $S$, that longer trace gets its own history with $T$ before
$S$. This inturn requires some discussion but prima facie looks okay.

Note that $H$ is built out of $\tau$ and nothing else, much like
$m := n + 1$ is the witness for $\forall n.\ \exists m.\ m > n$.


*** H1: $\forall T.\ H.\mathit{txs}\ T \leftrightarrow \mathit{txs}(\tau)\ T$

By construction: $H.\mathit{txs} := \mathit{txs}(\tau)$, so H1 is
$A \leftrightarrow A$.

*** H2: $S \neq T \rightarrow \mathit{AR}(S,T) \vee \mathit{AR}(T,S)$

Case on the status of $S$ and $T$.
(More details on the physical notebook)

*** H3: $\mathit{AR}(S,T) \rightarrow \neg \mathit{AR}(T,S)$
Still follows the same case analysis, shouldn't be difficult
- $\mathit{AR}(T,S)$ would put a pending transaction before a finished
  one.

*** H4: $\mathit{AR}(S,T) \wedge \mathit{AR}(T,U) \rightarrow \mathit{AR}(S,U)$
Case analysis, ditto
- pend, pend, pend: transitivity on $\mathsf{begin}$, using some unquiness.

*** H5: $S \neq T \wedge \mathit{RT}(S,T) \rightarrow \mathit{AR}(S,T)$

$\mathit{RT}(S,T)$ says $S$ ended before $T$ began, so $S$ is finished.
- $T$ pending: the middle clause.
- $T$ finished: needs $\mathit{begin}_T \prec \mathit{term}_T$; the LLM
  should be able to fill this in by induction on the run.

** Proving Internal Consistency

Cerone's internal-consistency axiom:

\begin{align*}
\forall (E, \mathit{po}) \in H.\ \forall e \in E.\ \forall x, n.\quad
  & \bigl(e = (\_, \mathsf{get}(x,n)) \wedge \mathit{po}^{-1}(e) \cap \mathit{HEvent}_x \neq \emptyset\bigr) \\
  & \rightarrow \max\nolimits_{\mathit{po}}\bigl(\mathit{po}^{-1}(e) \cap \mathit{HEvent}_x\bigr) = (\_, \mathsf{op}(x,n))
\end{align*}

where $\mathit{HEvent}_x$ is the set of $\mathsf{get}$ and $\mathsf{set}$ events
on $x$, and $\mathsf{op}(x,n)$ is a $\mathsf{get}$ or a $\mathsf{set}$ of $n$ on
$x$.

We had introduced $P_x(e)$ as a shorthand for
$\mathit{po}^{-1}(e) \cap \mathit{HEvent}_x$, so we reuse it here:

\begin{align*}
\forall (E, \mathit{po}) \in H.\ \forall e \in E.\ \forall x, n.\quad
  & \bigl(e = (\_, \mathsf{get}(x,n)) \wedge P_x(e) \neq \emptyset\bigr) \\
  & \rightarrow \max\nolimits_{\mathit{po}} P_x(e) = (\_, \mathsf{op}(x,n))
\end{align*}

*** The idea

Alright, now we have a log $\Lambda$ for every live transaction, same as in the
rules. A read $\mathsf{get}(x)$ inside $t$ fires one of two rules, and which
one depends only on $\Lambda(x)$:

\begin{align*}
\Lambda(x) = (o, c) &\;\Rightarrow\; \textsc{Get-Log},\ \text{returns } c \\
\Lambda(x) = \bot &\;\Rightarrow\; \textsc{Get-Mem},\ \text{returns } \sigma(x)
\end{align*}


*** (L) the log mirrors po

For every $C$ with $\mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C$, and every thread with
$\Theta(i) = (t, \Lambda, \hat E)$:

$$\Lambda(x) = \bot \;\leftrightarrow\; \tau \text{ has no access to } x \text{ tagged } t$$
$$\Lambda(x) = (o, c) \;\rightarrow\; \text{the latest access to } x \text{ tagged } t \text{ has value } c$$

Only live transactions; once $t$ commits or retries its log is gone
and (L) says nothing about it.

Induction on the run, $C_0 \xrightarrow{\tau} C \xrightarrow{a} C'$. Base:
$\Theta = \bot$ everywhere. Step, one case per rule:
- Begin: $\Lambda := \emptyset$, and $t$ is brand new so nothing is tagged $t$
  yet (needs: a fresh $t$ has no old events).
- Get-Mem, Set-Mem: $\Lambda(x) = \bot$ becomes $(\_, v)$; $a$ is the first
  access to $x$, value $v$.
- Set-Log: $(o, c)$ becomes $(o, v)$; $a$ is now the latest, value $v$.
- Get-Log: nothing changes; $a$ returns $c$ and is now the latest, value $c$.
- Commit, KCas-Retry, Retry: $\Theta(i) := \bot$, nothing to check.
- Other locations $y \neq x$: neither $\Lambda(y)$ nor the accesses to $y$
  change.
- Other threads: $a$ comes from $j$ running $s$; $t$'s log is untouched, and
  $a$ is not one of $t$'s accesses as long as $s \neq t$. So we need (D): two
  live threads never run the same $t$. Follows from $t$ fresh.

*** From (L) to Int

Induction again, $\mathit{Int}(\tau) \rightarrow \mathit{Int}(\tau \cdot a)$.
- $a$ is not a $\mathsf{get}$: nothing new to check.
- Old reads $e$: $P_x(e)$ only looks backwards and $a$ is later, so nothing
  changes for them.
- $a = \mathsf{get}(x, n)@t$ with $P_x(a) \neq \emptyset$:

\begin{align*}
P_x(a) \neq \emptyset
  &\;\Rightarrow\; t \text{ touched } x \\
  &\;\overset{(L)}{\Rightarrow}\; \Lambda(x) = (o, c) \\
  &\;\Rightarrow\; \text{not Get-Mem, so Get-Log and } n = c \\
  &\;\overset{(L)}{\Rightarrow}\; n = \text{value of } \max\nolimits_{\mathit{po}} P_x(a)
\end{align*}

What it rests on: (L) by induction, (D) live threads never share a $t$, and
(F) a fresh $t$ has no old events (tags below $\mathit{nextTx}$, already in
=inv_bbt=).

** Proving External Consistency

*** (a) Committed transactions

**** Statement

Fix a run $\mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C'$ and the history
$H = (\mathit{txs}(\tau), \mathit{AR})$ from [[*Witness][Witness]]. For a
transaction $T$ and a location $x$ write

\begin{align*}
T \vdash \mathsf{Read}(x, n) \;&:\Leftrightarrow\; \exists e \in T.\ e = (\_, \mathsf{get}(x, n)) \wedge \mathit{po}^{-1}(e) \cap \mathit{HEvent}_x = \emptyset \\
T \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, n) \;&:\Leftrightarrow\; \max\nolimits_{\mathit{po}}\{\, a \in T \mid a = (\_, \mathsf{set}(x, \_)) \,\} = (\_, \mathsf{set}(x, n)) \\
W_x(T) \;&:=\; \{\, S \in H \mid S \text{ committed},\ S \neq T,\ S \xrightarrow{\mathit{AR}} T,\ S \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, \_) \,\}
\end{align*}

*Theorem* ($\textsc{Ext}_c$).
\begin{align*}
\forall T \in H.\ T \text{ committed} \rightarrow \forall x, n.\ T \vdash \mathsf{Read}(x, n) \rightarrow\quad
  & \bigl(W_x(T) = \emptyset \wedge n = \sigma_0(x)\bigr) \\
  \vee\ & \max\nolimits_{\mathit{AR}} W_x(T) \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, n)
\end{align*}

This is Cerone's $\textsc{Ext}$ with $\mathit{VIS} = \mathit{AR}$, restricted
to committed readers. Uncommitted readers are [[*(b) Uncommitted transactions][part (b)]].

**** Lemmas

Throughout, $C$ ranges over configurations with $\mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C$,
and $\Theta(i) = (t, \Lambda, \hat E)$ is a live thread of $C$.

*Lemma 1* (External).
$$\Lambda(x) = \bot \;\leftrightarrow\; \tau \text{ has no access to } x \text{ tagged } t$$

/Proof./ Induction on the run, $C_0 \xrightarrow{\tau} C \xrightarrow{a} C'$.
Base: $\Theta = \bot$ everywhere, nothing to show. Step: fix $i$ live in $C'$
with $\Theta'(i) = (t, \Lambda', \hat E)$.

($\rightarrow$) Assume $\Lambda'(x) = \bot$; show $\tau \cdot a$ has no access
to $x$ tagged $t$.
- Begin at $i$: $\Lambda' = \emptyset$ and $t = \mathit{nextTx}$. Nothing in
  $\tau$ is tagged $t$ by (F); $a$ is not an access.
- Get-Mem, Set-Mem, Get-Log, Set-Log at $i$ on $x$: $\Lambda'(x) \neq \bot$,
  contradicting the assumption.
- Any of these at $i$ on $y \neq x$: $\Lambda(x) = \Lambda'(x) = \bot$, so the
  IH covers $\tau$, and $a$ is on $y$.
- Commit, KCas-Retry, Retry at $i$: $i$ is not live in $C'$.
- Any step at $j \neq i$: $\Lambda = \Lambda'$, so the IH covers $\tau$. $a$
  is tagged $j$'s transaction $s$, and $s \neq t$ by (D), or by (F) if $a$ is
  $\mathsf{begin}\ s$.
- $\mathsf{adm}$: no event, $\Theta$ unchanged.

($\leftarrow$) Assume $\tau \cdot a$ has no access to $x$ tagged $t$; show
$\Lambda'(x) = \bot$.
- Begin at $i$: $\Lambda' = \emptyset$.
- Get-Mem, Set-Mem, Get-Log, Set-Log at $i$ on $x$: then $a$ is an access to
  $x$ tagged $t$, contradicting the assumption.
- Any of these at $i$ on $y \neq x$: $\tau$ has no access to $x$ tagged $t$,
  so $\Lambda(x) = \bot$ by the IH, and $\Lambda'(x) = \Lambda(x)$.
- Commit, KCas-Retry, Retry at $i$: $i$ is not live in $C'$.
- Any step at $j \neq i$, and $\mathsf{adm}$: $\Lambda' = \Lambda$ and the IH
  applies to $\tau$. $\square$

*Lemma 2* (Expected value is kept). If $\Lambda(x) = (o, c)$ in $C$, and a
later $C''$ on the same run still has $\Theta''(i) = (t, \Lambda'', \_)$, then
$\Lambda''(x) = (o, c'')$ for some $c''$.

/Proof./ Induction on the steps between $C$ and $C''$. Only steps of $i$ change
$\Lambda$. Get-Mem and Set-Mem need $\Lambda(x) = \bot$, so they do not fire on
$x$. Set-Log replaces $(o, c)$ by $(o, v)$. Get-Log changes nothing. $\square$

*Lemma 3* (Current value). If $\Lambda(x) = (o, c)$ then
- $t$ has a $\mathsf{set}$ on $x$ in $\tau$ $\rightarrow$ $c$ is the value of
  $t$'s $\mathit{po}$-last $\mathsf{set}$ on $x$;
- $t$ has no $\mathsf{set}$ on $x$ in $\tau$ $\rightarrow$ $c = o$.

/Proof./ Induction on the run. The entry is created by Get-Mem as $(v, v)$, or
by Set-Mem as $(u, v)$ with $a = \mathsf{set}(x, v)$, and these are $t$'s first
access to $x$ (Lemma 1). Set-Log makes $c$ the value of the new last
$\mathsf{set}$. Get-Log changes neither $c$ nor the $\mathsf{set}$s. Steps of
other threads change neither, by (D). $\square$

*Lemma 4* (Store is the commit order). In every $C$,
$\sigma(x) = n$ where $S \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, n)$ for the latest $S$ that
commits in $\tau$ and has $S \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, \_)$, or
$\sigma(x) = \sigma_0(x)$ if there is no such $S$.

/Proof./ Induction on the run. Only Commit changes $\sigma$ or the set of
committed transactions. Let $t$ commit with $\Lambda$ and
$\sigma' = \mathit{apply}(\sigma, \Lambda)$.
- $\Lambda(x) = \bot$: $\sigma'(x) = \sigma(x)$, and $t$ has no access to $x$
  (Lemma 1), so the latest committed writer of $x$ is unchanged.
- $\Lambda(x) = (o, c)$ and $t$ set $x$: $\sigma'(x) = c$, which is $t$'s last
  $\mathsf{set}$ value (Lemma 3), and $t$ is now the latest committed writer.
- $\Lambda(x) = (o, c)$ and $t$ only read $x$: $c = o$ (Lemma 3) and
  $\sigma(x) = o$ (KCasOk), so $\sigma'(x) = \sigma(x)$, and $t$ is not a
  writer of $x$.

Events after a commit are never tagged with the committed transaction, by
(Closed), so $S \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, \_)$ does not change for a committed
$S$. $\square$

**** Proof of $\textsc{Ext}_c$

Let $T \in H$ be committed with transaction id $t$, and let
$e = \mathsf{get}(x, n)@t$ witness $T \vdash \mathsf{Read}(x, n)$. Let $c$ be
$t$'s commit event, and split the run at $e$ and at $c$:

$$C_0 \xrightarrow{\tau_1} C_e \xrightarrow{e} C_e' \longrightarrow \cdots \longrightarrow C_c \xrightarrow{c} C_c' \xrightarrow{\tau_3} C'$$

($e$ comes before $c$ by (Closed).)

1. /$e$ was fired by Get-Mem./ $e$ is emitted by the thread $i$ running $t$,
   with $\Theta_e(i) = (t, \Lambda_e, \_)$. $e$ is external, so $\tau_1$ has
   no access to $x$ tagged $t$, and $\Lambda_e(x) = \bot$ by Lemma 1
   ($\leftarrow$). Get-Log needs $\Lambda_e(x) \neq \bot$, so Get-Mem fired:
   $n = \sigma_e(x)$ and $\Lambda_e'(x) = (n, n)$.
2. /At commit, $t$ still expects $n$./ $i$ runs $t$ from $C_e'$ to $C_c$, so
   $\Lambda_c(x) = (n, \_)$ by Lemma 2.
3. /Memory agrees at commit./ Commit fired, so KCasOk gives
   $\sigma_c(x) = n$.
4. /Memory is the latest committed writer./ By Lemma 4 at $C_c$, $n$ is the
   value with $S \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, n)$ for the latest $S$ committed
   before $c$ that writes $x$, or $n = \sigma_0(x)$ if there is none.
5. /That writer is $\max_{\mathit{AR}} W_x(T)$./ For committed $S \neq T$,
   $S \xrightarrow{\mathit{AR}} T$ iff $\mathsf{term}_S \prec \mathsf{term}_T = c$
   (first clause of $\mathit{AR}$), that is, iff $S$ commits before $c$. So
   $W_x(T)$ is exactly the set of writers of $x$ committed before $c$. AR on
   committed transactions is commit order, so the latest one in step 4 is
   $\max_{\mathit{AR}} W_x(T)$, and "none" is $W_x(T) = \emptyset$. $\square$

**** What it rests on

- Lemmas 1–4 above, each by induction on the run.
- (F) a fresh $t$ has no old events; (D) two live threads never run the same
  $t$. Both are from the Int proof.
- (Closed) after $t$ commits or retries, no event is tagged $t$.
- The witness $\mathit{AR}$ and $\mathit{HistoryOf}\ \tau\ H$.

**** Design pattern (for later): state P on the event list, translate once

/Current Lean proof./ The P field of $\mathit{Inv}$ is stated on the witness
history: $P(\tau) := \textsc{Ext}_c(\mathit{witness}\ \tau)$. Every step then
rebuilds the transactions from scratch ($\mathit{transactionOf}\ (es \mathbin{+\!\!+} [n])\ t$)
and has to show the old ones are unchanged: B5
($\mathit{transactionOf}$ of an old tag is the same object), $W_x(T)$ and

$\mathit{tag}(n)$ never AR-before an old $T$. This works, and is kept for now.

/Alternative./ Cur, Store and R are stated on lists and logs. State P the same
way, on the event list, and move the history translation into the projection:

$$P_{\mathit{list}}(es) :\iff \forall t\ \text{committed},\ \forall\ \text{first read}\ \mathsf{get}(x,v)@t \in es,\quad
W = \{ s \mid \mathsf{end}_s \prec \mathsf{end}_t,\ s\ \text{sets}\ x \}$$
$$\quad (W = \emptyset \wedge v = \sigma_0(x)) \ \vee\ (s = \text{the}\ s \in W\ \text{with the latest}\ \mathsf{end}_s \wedge \mathit{wr}(s, x, v))$$

1. /Inv field/ $P_{\mathit{list}}$, one line per rule like Store: non-end

2. /Projection/ $P_{\mathit{list}}(\mathit{events}\ \tau) \rightarrow \textsc{Ext}_c(\mathit{witness}\ \tau)$,

Same architecture, $\mathit{Run} \rightarrow \mathit{Inv} \rightarrow P$; the

*** (b) Uncommitted transactions

$\textsc{Ext}$ over every $T \in H$, retried and pending included, fails for
our rules. A transaction can read a mixed snapshot before its k-CAS catches it
(a /zombie/), and no $\mathit{AR}$ places it. With $\sigma_0 = 0$:

#+begin_example
1  begin t
2  get x 0 @t     Get-Mem, σ(x) = 0
3  begin s
4  set x 1 @s
5  set y 1 @s
6  end s          KCasOk, σ = {x↦1, y↦1}
7  get y 1 @t     Get-Mem, σ(y) = 1
8  retry t        KCasFail: σ(x) = 1 ≠ 0
#+end_example

- $t \to s$: the read $y = 1$ has no committed writer of $y$ before $t$.
- $s \to t$: the read $x = 0$ should be $1$.

So the $\exists H$ statement fails too, not only the finish-order witness.
Under the lock this never arose: no other transaction could change memory
between $t$'s reads.

Keeping $\forall T$ (opacity) means changing the rules, for example by
validating at every read:

$$\textsc{Get-Mem}' \quad \Theta(i) = (t, \Lambda, \hat E),\ \Lambda(x) = \bot,\ \sigma(x) = v,\ \mathit{KCasOk}\ \sigma\ \Lambda$$

Open: as written, a $t$ whose check fails is stuck at the $\mathsf{get}$,
because KCas-Retry only fires on an $\mathsf{end}$ redex. Where should the retry go?

/Decision (2026-11-18)./ Opacity is not pursued. Read skew in an uncommitted
transaction is allowed: the zombie above is fine. Part (b) proves $\textsc{Ext}_u$
below, for the rules as they are.

**** Notation

$es = \mathit{events}(\tau)$, and a step appends one event: $es' = es \mathbin{+\!\!+} [n]$.

- $\mathit{Com}(es) = \{ s \mid \mathsf{end}_s \in es \}$, the committed tags.
- $\mathit{set}_{es}(s, x)$: $s$ has a $\mathsf{set}$ on $x$ in $es$.
  $\mathit{wr}_{es}(s, x, v)$: the last $\mathsf{set}$ of $s$ on $x$ writes $v$.
- $\mathit{Ext}_{es}(e)$: $e = \mathsf{get}(x, v)@t$ and no access of $t$ to $x$
  comes before $e$ in $es$ (the first access).
- Lemma B (part (a)), for $s \neq \mathit{tag}(n)$ and $a, b, e \in es$:
  B1 $a \prec_{es'} b \iff a \prec_{es} b$; B2 $\mathit{Ext}_{es'}(e) \iff \mathit{Ext}_{es}(e)$;
  B4 $\mathit{set}$ and $\mathit{wr}$ of $s$ unchanged. B5: the transaction of an
  old tag $s \neq \mathit{tag}(n)$ is unchanged.

**** Statement

Visibility is per operation. For an operation $e$ of $\tau$ (witness history $H$):

$$\mathit{VIS}(S, e) \;:\iff\; S \in \mathit{Com} \;\wedge\; \mathsf{end}_S \prec_\tau e$$
$$W_e \;:=\; \mathit{VIS}^{-1}(e) \cap \{ S \mid \mathit{set}(S, x) \} \qquad (e = \mathsf{get}(x, v))$$

*Theorem* ($\textsc{Ext}_u$). For every uncommitted $T \in H$ (retried or
pending) and every external read $e = \mathsf{get}(x, v)$ of $T$:

$$(W_e = \emptyset \wedge v = \sigma_0(x)) \;\vee\; (S = \max_{\mathit{AR}} W_e \wedge S \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, v))$$

Each read has its own $W_e$, so two reads of one $T$ may see different
committed prefixes (read skew). On committed transactions $\mathit{AR}$ is
commit order, so $\max_{\mathit{AR}} W_e$ is the writer in $W_e$ with the latest
$\mathsf{end}$.

/Strengthening./ We prove the same property for *every* $T$, committed
included, and call it $U(\tau)$. $\textsc{Ext}_u$ is $U$ restricted to
uncommitted $T$. The strengthening makes the Commit step free: a transaction
becoming committed creates no new obligation. (For committed $T$ it agrees with
part (a): by validation no writer of $x$ commits between $e$ and $\mathsf{end}_T$.)

**** The invariant

Part (a)'s $\mathit{Inv}$ gains two fields:

$$\mathit{Inv}_b(\tau, C) \;=\; \mathit{Inv}(\tau, C) \;\wedge\; L^-(\tau, C) \;\wedge\; U(\tau)$$

- $L^-$ (new; the other half of L; accepted 2026-11-18, in Lean as =logBack=). For every live $(t, \Lambda)$ in $C$:
  $$\Lambda(x) \neq \bot \;\Rightarrow\; \exists a \in es.\ \mathit{tag}(a) = t \wedge a\ \text{accesses}\ x$$
  With L: $\Lambda_t(x) = \bot \iff t$ has no access to $x$.
- $U$, as above.

$\mathit{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C \Rightarrow \mathit{Inv}_b(\tau, C)$ by induction on
the run, one table per field; then $\textsc{Ext}_u$ is the projection
$\mathit{Inv}_b \Rightarrow U \Rightarrow \textsc{Ext}_u$.

**** Lemma $L^-$

Two worlds, as for Cur: /mine/ is the stepping thread's log, /others/ every other
live log. Other logs never change, and $es \subseteq es'$, so an access that
witnessed $\Lambda(x) \neq \bot$ still does: others are framed on every row.

| rule        | mine                                                    | others |
|-------------+---------------------------------------------------------+--------|
| Begin       | new log $\Lambda = \emptyset$: nothing to show          | frame  |
| Get-Log     | $\Lambda$ unchanged                                     | frame  |
| Get-Mem $l$ | $\Lambda'(l) = (v,v)$; $n$ accesses $l$, $n \in es'$    | frame  |
| Set-Log $l$ | $\Lambda'(l) = (o,v)$; $\Lambda(l) \neq \bot$ before (IH) | frame  |
| Set-Mem $l$ | $\Lambda'(l) = (u,v)$; $n$ accesses $l$, $n \in es'$    | frame  |
| Commit      | leaves                                                  | frame  |
| KCas-Retry, Retry | leaves                                            | frame  |
| Adm         | no event                                                | frame  |

At $x \neq l$, $\Lambda'(x) = \Lambda(x)$ and the IH applies. $\square$

**** Lemma $U$

/Frame (old reads)./ Let $e \in es$, $e = \mathsf{get}(x, v)@t$ external.
Suppose $\mathit{tag}(n)$ has no terminator in $es$ (T for a live tag, F for a
fresh one). Then $e$'s obligation is the same in $es'$ as in $es$:

1. $\mathit{Ext}_{es'}(e) \iff \mathit{Ext}_{es}(e)$  (B2).
2. $W_e(es') = W_e(es)$. Let $s \in W_e(es')$, so $\mathsf{end}_s \prec_{es'} e$.
   Since $e \in es$ and $n$ is last, $\mathsf{end}_s \neq n$, so
   $\mathsf{end}_s \in es$ and $\mathsf{end}_s \prec_{es} e$ (B1). Then
   $s \in \mathit{Com}(es)$, so $s \neq \mathit{tag}(n)$, and $\mathit{set}$,
   $\mathit{wr}$ of $s$ are unchanged (B4). The converse is the same argument
   read backwards.
3. $\max_{\mathit{AR}}$ on $W_e$ is the latest $\mathsf{end}$, and the order of
   old $\mathsf{end}$s is unchanged (B1).
4. The writer's transaction is unchanged (B5), so $S \vdash \mathsf{Write}(x, v)$
   is unchanged.

/New read./ $n = \mathsf{get}(l, v)@t$ fired by Get-Mem, so

**** Open: is $\mathit{VIS} \subseteq \mathit{AR}$?

Cerone's VIS is a relation on transactions with $\mathit{VIS} \subseteq \mathit{AR}$.
Ours is heterogeneous ($\mathit{Tx} \times \mathit{Op}$), so the axiom has to be
restated. Candidate, lifting the operation to its transaction:

$$\mathit{VIS}(S, e) \;\Rightarrow\; S \neq \mathit{tx}(e) \;\wedge\; S \xrightarrow{\mathit{AR}} \mathit{tx}(e)$$

Sketch for the witness. $S \in \mathit{Com}$ and $\mathsf{end}_S \prec e$.
- $S \neq \mathit{tx}(e)$: $\mathsf{end}_S \prec e$ and no event of $S$ follows its $\mathsf{end}$ (Lemma N).
- $\mathit{tx}(e)$ finished, terminator $f$: $e \prec f$ (Lemma N), so
  $\mathsf{end}_S \prec e \prec f$, AR's first clause.
- $\mathit{tx}(e)$ pending: $S$ finished, $\mathit{tx}(e)$ not, AR's second clause.

   (commit order), but it is a separate axiom.

   condition on $H$), or a separate lemma about the witness?
