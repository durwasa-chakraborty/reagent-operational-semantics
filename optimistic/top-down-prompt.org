* Purpose

Rebuild the Ext proof top down, adding one invariant clause at a time, each
time a step case fails. Lean side: =Optimistic/TopDown.lean=.

* Where we are (pin)

** The theorem

\[ \mathrm{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C \implies \exists H.\ \mathrm{HistoryOf}(\tau, H) \land \mathrm{Int}(H) \land \mathrm{Ext}(H) \]

- $H$ is fixed once: $H := \mathit{witness}(\tau)$ (finish order). The $\exists$
  is introduced only at the end, so all three conjuncts are about the same $H$.
- $\mathrm{HistoryOf}$ and $\mathrm{Int}$ are *assumed* here (proved elsewhere).
- $\mathrm{Ext} = \mathrm{Ext}_c \land \mathrm{Ext}_u$. We work on $\mathrm{Ext}_c$ first.

** Strategy

Ext alone does not survive a step (it says nothing about $\sigma$ or the logs),
so strengthen:

- (A) $\mathrm{Run}\ C_0\ \tau\ C \implies \mathrm{Inv}(\tau, C)$, by induction on the run
- (B) $\mathrm{Inv}(\tau, C) \implies \mathrm{Ext}_c(\mathit{witness}\ \tau)$, by projection

$\mathrm{Inv} := \mathit{true}$ makes (A) trivial and (B) impossible. Start from
the smallest $\mathrm{Inv}$ that gives (B), and grow it.

** Round 1: $\mathrm{Inv}(\tau, C) := P(\tau)$

$P(\tau) = \mathrm{Ext}_c(\mathit{witness}\ \tau)$. In English: every committed
transaction $T$ reads, from outside itself, the value left by the last
committed transaction before it (in finish order) that wrote that location,
or the initial value if there is none.

- External read: a =get x= in $T$ with no earlier access to =x= by $T$
  (otherwise it is Int's business).
- Writers: only committed $S$ count; a retried $S$ never reached memory.

Example: =[begin t0, set x 5 t0, end t0, begin t1, get x 5 t1, end t1]=
— $T_1$ reads 5, the last committed writer before it is $T_0$ with 5. ✓

*** PROVED Base case: $P([])$

Vacuous: =witness []= has no transactions, and $\forall T \in \emptyset.\ \ldots = \mathit{true}$.

*** PROVED Step, =begin t=

Nothing commits and the finish order does not change, so $P$ carries over.

Gap found while mechanizing: this silently uses that $t$ is *fresh*
(no earlier event of $\tau$ carries tag $t$; $t =$ =nextTx=) and that event
ids are unique. Without freshness, =begin t= could re-open an already
committed $t$ and change that transaction. In Lean these come from the
standalone lemmas =inv_bbt= (F) and =inv_ids= (Ids), not from $P$.
Candidate clause: (F) "every tag in $\tau$ is below =nextTx=".

*** OPEN Step, =get=, =set=

$P$ only looks at committed transactions; the reader $t$ is live. We do not
know yet whether $t$ will commit, so whatever we say must hold either way.

*** OPEN Step, =end t= (commit)

First real trouble expected: $t$ becomes committed, and all its earlier reads
suddenly have to satisfy $P$. Something must have remembered what $t$ read.
